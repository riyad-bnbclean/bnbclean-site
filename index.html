Questions, answered.

What happens if my cleaner cancels?

We immediately re-list the job to our network of cleaners in your area, so someone else can pick it up. You'll be notified straight away.

How do I pay?

Securely in the app, by card, through Stripe. You only pay once you've chosen your cleaner.

I manage several properties. Can I pay weekly?

Yes. Ask us about a Premium account: book without paying upfront, one statement every Sunday.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled time.

Are the cleaners insured?

Our cleaners are independent contractors with their own ABN. We recommend hosts check their cleaner's profile and reviews before booking.

Do cleaners bring their own equipment?

Some do, and you'll see it on their profile. Otherwise, they'll use what you provide.

What's the "duo" option?

Two cleaners on the same job. Ideal for large properties or tight turnarounds between guests.

Which areas do you cover?

Cairns, Palm Cove, Port Douglas and surrounding suburbs. Join our waitlist if you're elsewhere.
