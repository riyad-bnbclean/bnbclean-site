Our story

Built here, for people here.

BNB Clean started with a simple frustration: hosts couldn't find reliable cleaners, and good cleaners couldn't find steady, fairly paid work.

We're a small, local operation based in Far North Queensland. We're new, and we're building our reputation one clean at a time. That means every job matters to us, and every cleaner on the platform is someone we'd trust with our own place.

No call centres. No faceless corporation. Just people who care about doing this properly.

Riyad, founder

Install

Install it like a real app

Add BNB Clean to your home screen in seconds, no app store needed.

On iPhone

  1. 1Open app.bnbclean.au in Safari.
  2. 2Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. 3Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

On Android

  1. 1Open app.bnbclean.au in Chrome.
  2. 2Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. 3Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then confirm.
On Samsung phones, this also works in the Samsung Internet browser: tap the menu, then "Add page to" → "Home screen".
